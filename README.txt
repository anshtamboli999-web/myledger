MY LEDGER - standalone app files

1. Go to https://app.netlify.com/drop and drag this whole folder (unzipped) onto the page.
   You get a free https link.
2. Open that link in Chrome on your Android phone, tap the menu, then "Install app".
   It now works like a normal app, even offline (after the first open).
3. Optional - real APK: go to https://www.pwabuilder.com, paste your link, choose Android.

Before moving: in the old version go to More > Copy backup. In the new app go to More > Restore.
